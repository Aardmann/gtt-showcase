1. Who We Are

Ghana Trotro Transit is built and operated by NxNx Tech, CC416 Adjei Kwashi Street, Accra, Ghana. GTT is an independently developed transit-information tool and is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or operated by GPRTU, any government agency, or any trotro operator. NxNx Tech is the data controller responsible for the personal information described in this Policy.

2. Information We Collect

2.1 Information You Provide

  • Feedback and bug reports: When you use the feedback form, you may provide your name, a message, and a feedback category (bug report, feature request, route correction, compliment, or general feedback). Submitting feedback opens WhatsApp and sends your message, along with your WhatsApp phone number, directly to our support line.
  • Account registration (optional): You never need an account to search routes, view fares, or use offline maps signing in is entirely optional. If you choose to create an account, we collect only your name and email address to set up, secure, and identify your account. Where we offer sign-in through a third-party identity provider, we receive only the basic profile information that provider shares with us (typically your name and email) to authenticate you we do not receive your third-party password, and we do not access your third-party account beyond that basic profile information.
  • Custom or saved routes: If you create, save, or share a custom route, we store the route details (origin, destination, stops) you enter. If you're signed in, these are linked to your account and synced so they're available across your devices; if you're not signed in, they stay stored locally on your device only.

2.2 Location Data

Location access is optional and only used when you enable it. If granted, we use your device's GPS coordinates to show nearby trotro stations, calculate route suggestions, and provide live navigation prompts and stop alerts. Location data is processed on-device or transmitted only as needed to generate route results, and is not compiled into a persistent history of your movements or sold to third parties.

2.3 Usage and Device Data

We automatically collect limited technical data to keep the Service working reliably, including:

  • Device model, operating system and version (Android, iOS, or browser/OS for the web app)
  • App version (e.g. Beta 4)
  • General usage data such as routes searched, screens viewed, and crash/error logs
  • IP address and network-level information when using the web app
  • A device or session identifier used to keep the app working consistently, and where you have an account to associate that device with your account

2.4 Offline and Local Storage

To support offline navigation, the app caches route maps, saved routes, and favourites locally on your device in sandboxed app storage. This cached data stays on your device and is used to let you view routes without a data connection.

3. How We Use Information

  • To find and display trotro routes, stops, and fare estimates
  • To provide real-time guidance, stop notifications, and offline route access
  • To create and authenticate your optional account, and keep your saved routes and favourites in sync across devices when you're signed in
  • To respond to feedback, bug reports, and route corrections
  • To improve route accuracy using aggregated, community-contributed route data
  • To maintain app stability, prevent abuse, and diagnose technical issues

4. Legal Basis for Processing

Where required by applicable data protection law, we rely on the following bases to process your personal information:

  • Consent for example, when you enable location access, choose to create an account, or submit feedback.
  • Performance of a contract to provide features you've asked for, such as syncing saved routes once you register.
  • Legitimate interests to secure, maintain, and improve the Service, prevent misuse, and understand aggregate usage, provided these interests are not overridden by your rights and interests.
  • Legal obligation where processing is necessary to comply with Ghanaian law or another applicable legal requirement.

Users in Ghana are additionally protected by the Data Protection Act, 2012 (Act 843), and we aim to follow the data protection principles set out by Ghana's Data Protection Commission.

5. Fare and Route Data Sources

Fare estimates referenced as "GPRTU-approved" are based on publicly available fare information and are provided for guidance only. Actual fares, routes, and schedules may vary due to traffic, road conditions, fuel prices, or individual driver/operator decisions, and are not guaranteed to be accurate at the time of your trip.

6. How We Share Information

We do not sell your personal information. We may share limited information in the following circumstances:

  • Feedback via WhatsApp: messages sent through our feedback form are transmitted via WhatsApp/Meta's messaging infrastructure and are subject to WhatsApp's own privacy practices.
  • Backend and infrastructure providers: we use third-party infrastructure providers including our database and backend platform, Supabase to securely store account information, saved routes, and route/location data. These providers process data only on our behalf and under contractual restrictions.
  • Service providers: hosting, analytics, or crash-reporting providers that help us operate the Service, bound to use data only for that purpose.
  • Legal requirements: where required to comply with Ghanaian law, respond to lawful requests, or protect the rights, safety, or property of GTT or its users.
  • Business transfers: if NxNx Tech is ever involved in a merger, acquisition, or sale of assets, your information may be transferred as part of that transaction; we'll notify you of any such change and any resulting choices you may have.

7. International Data Storage & Transfers

Our infrastructure providers may store and process information on servers located outside Ghana. Where this happens, we take reasonable steps including contractual safeguards with our providers to keep your information protected to a standard consistent with this Privacy Policy, regardless of where it is processed.

8. Mobile App Permissions

  • Location (optional): used only for route search, nearby stations, and live navigation, and only after you grant permission via your device settings.
  • Push notifications: used for stop alerts, route updates, and service announcements. You can disable these at any time in your device settings.
  • Local storage: used to cache maps and routes for offline use, kept in an encrypted, sandboxed app folder inaccessible to other apps.
  • Internet connection: required to fetch live route and fare data, sign in, and sync your account; the app falls back to cached offline data when unavailable.

You can review or revoke any of these permissions at any time through your device's operating system privacy settings.

9. Cookies & Similar Technologies (Web App)

The web version of GTT may use cookies or similar local storage technologies to remember your preferences, keep you signed in, and store saved routes within your browser. You can clear or block cookies through your browser settings, though this may sign you out or limit some features.

10. Data Retention

We retain feedback and usage data only as long as necessary to operate and improve the Service, respond to your requests, and comply with legal obligations. If you have an account, we retain your name, email address, and account-linked data for as long as your account stays active; see Section 14 for what happens when you delete it. Locally cached offline data remains on your device until you clear the app's storage or uninstall the app.

11. Data Security

We take reasonable technical and organizational measures including encrypted local storage and access-controlled backend infrastructure to protect information from unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure, or destruction. However, no method of transmission or storage is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

12. Children's Privacy

The Service is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information including account registrations from children under 13. If you believe a child has created an account or otherwise provided us with personal information, please contact us so we can delete it and, where applicable, deactivate the account.

13. Your Privacy Rights and Choices

Depending on your location, you may have some or all of the following rights over your personal information:

  • Access request a copy of the personal information we hold about you.
  • Correction ask us to correct inaccurate or incomplete information, such as your account name or email.
  • Deletion request deletion of your account and associated personal information (see Section 14).
  • Portability request your data in a portable format, where technically feasible.
  • Objection or restriction object to, or ask us to restrict, certain processing, such as location use.
  • Withdraw consent withdraw previously given consent (for example, by disabling location or notification permissions) at any time, without affecting processing that already took place.

You can also decline location, notification, or storage permissions at any time via your device settings, and you can stop using the Service and uninstall the app at any time, which removes locally cached data from your device. To exercise any of these rights, contact us using the details in Section 20 we may need to verify your identity first.

14. Account Management & Deletion

If you've created an account, you can request its deletion at any time either through an in-app account deletion option, where available, or by emailing us at the address in Section 20. Once we've verified your request, we will delete your name, email address, and account-linked saved routes within a reasonable period, except where we're legally required to keep certain records. Deleting your account does not automatically remove information you've separately shared with third parties (for example, a feedback message sent via WhatsApp) or data cached locally on your device, which you can clear by uninstalling the app.

15. Do Not Sell or Share My Information

We do not sell your personal information, and we do not share it with third parties for their own advertising purposes. If this ever changes, we will update this Privacy Policy in advance and, where required by law, provide a way to opt out.

16. Automated Decision-Making

We don't use your personal information to make decisions that have a legal or similarly significant effect on you through fully automated means. Route and fare suggestions are informational only and aren't a form of automated decision-making about you as an individual.

17. Third-Party Links
